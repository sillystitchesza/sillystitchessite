Hello, I'm so glad
you're here

Silly Stitches started in my room, with a single crochet hook, a ball of pink yarn, and many mistakes. What began as a way to slow down after a long day at work turned into a passion — and eventually, a little shop. We began with crotchet, and we continue with sewing!

Every piece I make is worked by hand, stitch by stitch. I source soft, natural fibres and patterns and take my time with every project. No rush. No mass production. Just a silly, cozy craft.

Thank you for supporting handmade. It truly means the world. 🌿

— Emma x
